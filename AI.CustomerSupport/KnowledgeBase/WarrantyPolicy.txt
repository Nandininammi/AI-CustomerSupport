COMPANY WARRANTY POLICY

1. Warranty Period
All laptops purchased from our company have a 2-year warranty.

2. What Is Covered
The warranty covers:
- Hardware manufacturing defects
- Keyboard defects
- Screen defects
- Battery defects during the warranty period

3. What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
- Accidental physical damage
- Liquid damage
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear

4. Warranty Claim
Customers must provide their original purchase invoice when making a warranty claim.

5. Repair Time
Standard warranty repairs normally take 5 to 7 business days.

6. Customer Support
Customers can contact support Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
